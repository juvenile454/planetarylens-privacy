Privacy policy for PlanetaryLens

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy applies to PlanetaryLens 0.9, 0.9.1, 0.9.2, 1.0 and 1.0.1. It explains existing direct provider requests in more detail. Build 1.0.1 carries forward the disabling of the automatic emoji-font request through an Android system service already prepared for the revised 1.0 build; it adds no analytics or advertising features.

What the app does

PlanetaryLens displays Earth observations, camera stills, timelapses and wildlife photos. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not create usage profiles or run a user database. Network requests still disclose connection data to the services delivering the content.

The app needs Internet permission, but no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or media permission. We do not sell user data.

Data on your device

Saved sources are stored as catalog IDs in private app storage. Searches, map selections and the current screen are processed locally; Android may retain screen state to restore the app. Searches, saved sources and device location are not sent to providers. Your primary device language is read locally: German selects German, every other language selects English. It is not sent as a user preference.

The most recently downloaded source-control manifest is stored locally only if a source-control URL is configured. It contains source IDs, provider names and public notices, not user identifiers. It is replaced after a successful refresh. The current configuration is described under “Source controls and policy hosting”. App cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Images, video buffers and observation feeds are held in memory without a persistent media cache. Closing a view releases its media; Android manages final memory reclamation.

Remove saved items with the star or clear the saved list under About. To remove all local data, use Android Settings → Apps → PlanetaryLens → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. No account deletion is needed because no account exists.

Connections and recipients

Displaying previews in Discover and Catalog can connect to image providers as soon as a preview is visible, including on the start screen. Opening a source loads its image; current camera images can refresh while visible. Clips and timelapses start only when you tap play; previously selected playback may resume after the view is restored. World loads the enabled measurement layers. Earthquakes, fire and wind are enabled on the first visit through the tab; entering through a tile or the Earth view may activate a different layer selection. Switching a layer off, leaving World or putting the app in the background stops measurement-layer polling. An in-flight request can finish within its network timeout. Pausing a video does not guarantee that the Android player immediately stops every buffering request; closing the video view releases the player and its data source.

The app uses only HTTPS with transport encryption for its content and data requests and restricts destination hosts. The service receives your IP address, request time, the requested resource and a shared technical User-Agent identifying PlanetaryLens and its version. These requests contain no advertising ID, installation ID, account ID, GPS position, search text or saved list. The selected resource can reveal which content you are viewing. The app does not intentionally set cookies or send browser cookies. Providers and their delivery infrastructure may retain server logs under their own policies and use them for purposes including security, operation and statistical analysis. PlanetaryLens cannot access these logs, and deleting local app data does not erase them.

Media recipients: U.S. Geological Survey (USGS NIMS/HIVIS and the Hawaiian, Alaska, Cascades and Yellowstone Volcano Observatories, including USGS Amazon S3 and volcview.wr.usgs.gov image delivery); PhenoCam Network / Northern Arizona University; GeoNet / Earth Sciences New Zealand; iNaturalist Open Data through Amazon S3. Provider attribution and source/rights links appear in every detail view.

World-layer recipients: USGS for earthquakes; NOAA NDBC and PacIOOS / University of Hawaiʻi for buoy measurements and wave models; NOAA Aviation Weather Center for wind; GitHub for the prepared NASA FIRMS fire feed; NOAA CO-OPS, USGS Water Services, PEGELONLINE / German Federal Waterways and Shipping Administration, Environment and Climate Change Canada, Environment Agency (England), and Hub’Eau / Eaufrance for water levels. Fire data use a fixed public GitHub URL. The NASA preparation job receives no requests from your phone; its API key is never included in the app. GitHub describes deriving a general geographic region from IP addresses in its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . PlanetaryLens itself does not calculate your location.

The fire feed can contain VIIRS observations from NOAA-20/21 or fallback MODIS observations from Terra/Aqua. Both are prepared through NASA FIRMS exclusively by the existing server job. The app requests fixed public GitHub feed files; during the transition to the new format, an HTTP 404 response can cause an additional request for the previous fixed file. Map viewport, searches, device location and user identifiers are not transmitted. Changing the sensor does not add a recipient of phone connection data. Fire points and source status are held only in memory.

The globe texture, borders and capitals are bundled locally and do not contact a map or tile service. Source locations identify the observation site, not your phone.

Source controls and policy hosting

No source-control URL is configured in this build; no control file is requested or stored.

When a manifest URL is configured, the app requests a small HTTPS file on entering the foreground and about every 15 minutes while in the foreground. It can remove sources and show notices, without downloading executable code or adding sources. The host receives the connection data described above. Offline, the last valid file continues to apply; immediate removal on an offline device cannot be guaranteed. Viewing this policy inside the app works offline. Opening its public web version contacts its host through your browser.

The public version of this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes the connection data needed to deliver and protect the page under its own policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . We do not control GitHub's server-log retention and do not claim that these requests are anonymous.

The public page uses no analytics tools, cookies, external fonts or external scripts added by us. GitHub Pages logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes regardless of whether they are signed into GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages#data-collection .

Contract-related requests rely on Article 6(1)(b), other support and complaints on Article 6(1)(f), and legally required records on Article 6(1)(c). Do not include unnecessary sensitive data. Correspondence is deleted after the request is resolved and no longer needed, unless an applicable retention duty or a legal dispute requires continued storage.

Optional information and necessary connection data

You do not need to create an account or provide us with contact details to use the app. There is no legal or contractual obligation to supply us with voluntary contact details or attachments. Technical connection data are nevertheless necessary for the online features you use: without their transmission, external images, videos and current observations cannot be loaded. The bundled Earth, legal documents and local saved list remain available offline. Contacting us and providing attachments are voluntary; without a reachable contact address, we cannot answer an email request.

The US decision covers only commercial organisations participating in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; it is not general recognition for USGS, NOAA or public universities. PEGELONLINE and Hub’Eau are provided by organisations in Germany and France respectively. A provider's classification is not a guarantee that every part of its delivery infrastructure is in the same country. Information about these arrangements and privacy rights is available through GitHub's privacy contact. These statements do not automatically cover independent content providers, even if they use GitHub or Amazon infrastructure. For direct requests to US/Canadian public authorities, US public universities and iNaturalist, we do not claim blanket coverage by the Data Privacy Framework or by standard contractual clauses concluded by us.

Right to object

You may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to the processing of your personal data based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Contact our privacy address above and describe the processing concerned. We will stop that processing unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds overriding your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing is necessary for establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. We do not process your data for direct marketing.

Android system services

Android may process system diagnostics according to its own settings. PlanetaryLens runs no telemetry service for this purpose. In older app versions, including the original 1.0 build, the AndroidX library may ask a system service for an emoji font; any font download by that service is subject to the device or system provider's settings and privacy information. Disabling this was prepared in the revised 1.0 build; this automatic initialization is disabled in build 1.0.1. This is not a guarantee concerning all connections that the operating system makes independently of PlanetaryLens.

External links, children and changes

Provider pages and email links open an external app. That app and the destination service have their own privacy practices. PlanetaryLens is intended for a general audience interested in Earth observations, and is not specifically directed at children. There is no user upload, chat or communication between users. We do not perform automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

We update this policy when the app or data handling changes and show the revision date above. The public version is available at https://juvenile454.github.io/planetarylens-privacy/.
